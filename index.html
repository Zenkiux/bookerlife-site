The real thing, on your iPhone and Mac.

  • Real sizes. Books use their real or estimated height, and the shelf adds up its length in centimetres.

Make every reading moment count.

Log pages in two taps or run a reading session with a timer. Track print and audiobooks, even both for one book, and watch your goal move with you.

  • Goals. Books, pages or minutes, yearly or monthly, with pace and a forecast.
  • Every reading kept. Rereads, pauses and did-not-finish, with quarter-star ratings.

Your library belongs to you.

BookerLife keeps your reading on your devices. There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing is sent to us.

No BookerLife account

There is nothing to sign up for.

No BookerLife servers

We never receive your books, readings or notes.

No analytics or ads

No tracking of any kind.

Stored on your device

Your library lives on your iPhone or Mac, with daily local snapshots you can restore.

Your own iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync, your library syncs through your private iCloud database, which only you can read.

Export any time

Everything as JSON or a Goodreads-style CSV, free.

To find books, covers and descriptions, the app contacts Open Library, Wikidata and Wikipedia directly from your device (and Apple’s iTunes Search in builds where it is turned on), sending only the ISBN, title, author or search words involved. Read the full Privacy Policy
