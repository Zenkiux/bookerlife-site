BookerLife

A reading tracker for iPhone and Mac, built around a bookshelf that looks like yours: every book at its real size, with its real cover.

Log pages in two taps, keep every reread, write down the lines you want to remember, and see your year in books. Bring your history from Goodreads or StoryGraph, and export everything whenever you like.

Your library stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. There is no BookerLife account and no server.

  • PrivacyWhat the app collects: nothing
  • SupportImport, sync, purchases, contact
BookerLife is an independent app for iPhone and Mac. No account, no ads, no tracking.
