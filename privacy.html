Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. BookerLife has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. We, the makers, never receive your books, readings, notes or any other data.

Where your data lives

Your library is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud, it syncs through your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Book information

To find books, covers and descriptions, the app asks these services directly from your device, sending only the ISBN, title, author or search words involved: Open Library (openlibrary.org), Wikidata (wikidata.org) and Wikipedia (wikipedia.org), and, in builds where it is turned on, Apple's iTunes Search service (apple.com). Nothing else about you or your library is sent, and nothing passes through us. Each service has its own privacy policy. Covers and descriptions are cached on your device. Purchases

BookerLife Plus is sold by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Diagnostics

If you share crash reports with developers in your Apple settings, Apple passes anonymous crash data to us. You can turn this off at any time.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all my data removes everything from the device and from your iCloud. Export first if you want a copy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
