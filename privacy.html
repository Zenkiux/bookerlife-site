Account

Your library
On your device, and in your private iCloud if you turn sync on.

Where your data lives #

Your library is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud in Settings, it syncs through your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. Apple’s privacy policy covers iCloud.

Looking up books #

To find books, covers and descriptions, the app asks these services directly from your device, sending only the ISBN, title, author or search words involved: Open Library (external site), Wikidata (external site) and Wikipedia (external site), and, in builds where it is turned on, Apple’s iTunes Search service. Nothing else about you or your library is sent, and nothing passes through us. Each service has its own privacy policy. Covers and descriptions are kept on your device. Text from Wikipedia is shown with its source under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

On-device features #

Scanning a barcode or a page of text happens on your device with Apple’s Vision framework; the photo of a page is deleted once you have chosen the lines to keep. Reminders, widgets and the reading timer run on your device.

Purchases #

BookerLife Plus is sold by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Diagnostics #

If you share crash reports with developers in your Apple settings, Apple passes anonymous crash data to us. You can turn this off at any time in your device’s settings.

Deleting your data #

Settings → Delete all my data removes everything from the device and from your iCloud. Export first if you want a copy.

Children #

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact #

Questions about privacy are welcome on the support page.

BookerLife is an independent app for iPhone and Mac. No account, no ads, no tracking.
