Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours isn't here, get in touch at the end of the page.

Bring your books from Goodreads or StoryGraph

Export a CSV from Goodreads (My Books → Import and export) or StoryGraph (Manage account → Export). In BookerLife, open Settings → Import, pick the file, check the preview, then import. Covers are filled in on their own afterwards.

Sync between iPhone and Mac

Sign in to the same Apple account with iCloud on, on each device, then turn on Settings → iCloud in BookerLife on each one. The first sync shows a review list before anything is merged, and it can be undone for 30 days.

Fix a wrong cover or edition

Open the book, choose the Details tab, then tap Edition to pick another edition or correct the title, author and page count. Library Health, under Library, lists books with missing details and possible duplicates.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → BookerLife Plus → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple account you bought with. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple account's subscription settings.

Export or delete your data

Settings → Export saves everything as JSON or a Goodreads-style CSV. Settings → Delete all my data removes it from the device and your iCloud.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Zenkiux/bookerlife-site/issues. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.

BookerLife is an independent app for iPhone and Mac. No account, no ads, no tracking.
