Support

Bring books from Goodreads or StoryGraph

Export a CSV from Goodreads (My Books → Import and export) or StoryGraph (Manage account → Export). In BookerLife, open Settings → Import, pick the file, check the preview, then import.

Sync between devices

Sign in to the same Apple account with iCloud on, on each device, then turn on Settings → iCloud in BookerLife on each one. The first sync shows a review list before anything is merged. BookerLife syncs through your private iCloud database.

Restore a purchase

Settings → BookerLife Plus → Restore purchases.
