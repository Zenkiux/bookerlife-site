Getting started

Add a book

Open Discover and search by title, author or ISBN. Under Quick add, Scan barcode reads a book’s barcode with the camera, and Add by hand lets you enter a book yourself, with a photo of its cover.

Book details come from Open Library. Barcode scanning happens on your device.

Related: The book I want isn’t found

Log your reading

On Today, tap Log next to the book you are reading to record the page you reached or a percentage. Tap Continue reading to start a reading session with a timer instead; when you stop, the session is saved with the pages you read.

Every action can be undone.

Related: Widgets and the Live Activity

Set a reading goal

Open Settings → Yearly goal under Reading. A goal can count books, pages or minutes, for the year or for a month, and shows your pace and a forecast.

Streaks and badges are opt-in. Challenges only count up; nothing is ever taken away for missing a day.

Importing books

Bring your books from Goodreads or StoryGraph

Export a CSV from Goodreads (My Books → Import and export) or StoryGraph (Manage account → Export). In BookerLife, open Settings → Import from Goodreads or StoryGraph, pick the file, check the preview, then import. Covers are filled in on their own afterwards.

Related: A cover is missing or wrong

Import your Kindle highlights

Open Settings → Import from Goodreads or StoryGraph, then use Choose a File… on the Highlights from Kindle card. BookerLife reads a Kindle My Clippings.txt file, a Kindle notebook export (HTML) or a highlights CSV. You see a preview first, highlights already in your journal are skipped, and each import can be undone.

Library and editions

Fix a wrong cover or edition

Open the book, choose the Details tab, then tap Edition to pick another edition or correct the title, author and page count. Library Health, under Library, lists books with missing details and possible duplicates.

Related: Find missing details and duplicates

Find missing details and duplicates

Library Health, under Library, finds books with missing details and possible duplicates. You decide every fix, and every change can be undone.

Lend a book and get a reminder

Open the book and choose My copy, then Lend. Add who has it and, if you like, when it is due back; turn on Remind me on the due date for a reminder. The book keeps a history of its loans.

Reading and progress

Track an audiobook, or print and audio together

One reading can mix print and audio, each with its own length. Pages come only from print sessions and listening time only from audio sessions, and the book is counted once in your stats. Listening-speed options show the time left at your speed.

Rereads, pauses and books you didn’t finish

Each time you read a book is kept as its own reading, with its own dates, rating and review. You can also pause a book or mark it as did-not-finish with a reason. Ratings go in quarter stars.

Widgets and the Live Activity

On iPhone, the Reading Now widget shows your current book and can add ten pages with a tap. While a reading session runs, a Live Activity shows the timer on the Lock Screen, with a Stop button. Add widgets the usual way: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose BookerLife.

The Reading Goal widget is part of BookerLife Plus.

iCloud sync

Sync between iPhone and Mac

Sign in to the same Apple account with iCloud on, on each device, then turn on Settings → iCloud Sync in BookerLife on each one. The first sync shows a review list before anything is merged, and it can be undone for 30 days.

Related: Undo the first sync, Books aren’t syncing

Undo the first sync

Open Settings → iCloud Sync and choose Undo First Sync…, available for 30 days after the first sync. This device goes back to the library it had before the first sync, and iCloud sync turns off. What is in iCloud stays as it is.

BookerLife Plus and purchases

Restore a purchase or cancel a subscription

Open Settings → BookerLife Plus and tap Restore while signed in to the Apple account you bought with. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple account’s subscription settings.

What stays free, and what happens if Plus ends

Books, search, scanning, import and export, backups, iCloud sync, reading sessions, every Insights chart, goals, the journal and one shelf are free. Plus adds more shelves and looks, smart collections, reading plans, unlimited quote scans and challenges, more ambient sounds and the full Wrapped pack.

If Plus ends, nothing you made is deleted.

Exporting and deleting your data

Export or delete your data

In Settings, Export everything saves your whole library as JSON, and Export as Goodreads CSV saves a spreadsheet other apps can import. Settings → Delete All Data removes it from the device and your iCloud; you type DELETE to confirm. Export first if you want a copy.

Related: Privacy Policy: deleting your data

Get back something you deleted

Deleted books stay in Settings → Recently Deleted for 30 days, where you can restore them. BookerLife also keeps daily snapshots on your device; pick one under Restore a snapshot in Settings to take your library back to it.

Troubleshooting

A cover is missing or wrong

Covers are looked up on their own and can take a little while after an import, especially for a large library. If a book still has no cover or the wrong one, pick another edition.

Related: Fix a wrong cover or edition

The book I want isn’t found

Try the ISBN from the back of the book, or scan its barcode. If it still isn’t found, use Add by hand in Discover and add a photo of the cover.

Related: Add a book

Books aren’t syncing between devices

Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple account with iCloud on, and that Settings → iCloud Sync is turned on in BookerLife on each one. Sync starts the next time you open BookerLife, so close and reopen the app after turning it on.
